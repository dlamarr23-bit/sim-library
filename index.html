Topic 4

Energy Transfer via Friction

How Friction Affects Speed Roll a car down a ramp onto four surfaces and time how far it goes. Does Friction Create Heat Rub your hands together and measure how much warmer they get. Determining How Mass Affects Friction Load a tray with masses and compare the starting and sliding force. Kinetic Energy and Stopping Distance Release a car from different heights and see how far it pushes a box. Friction Rub two books together and watch atoms heat up and break off.

Mass and Acceleration

Acceleration Carry a string accelerometer as you speed up, slow down and turn. Acceleration on a Ramp Change the ramp and cart, then use photogates to find the acceleration. Forces in 1 Dimension Push a cabinet, crate or fridge and graph how it accelerates.

Collisions and Newton's Third Law

Poker Chip Inertia Slide chips into a stack and count how many get knocked out. Balloon Rocket Blow up balloons of different sizes and measure how far they fly. Inertia and Collisions Tap a rolling lab car and watch what a loose poker chip does. Crumple Zone Lab Design crumple zones, seat belts and airbags, then crash test the car. Newton's 3rd Law: Spring Scale Pull Pull two spring scales against each other and compare the forces. Collision Lab Crash balls of different masses and see momentum conserved.
