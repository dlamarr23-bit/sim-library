How to use this sim

  1. Choose a substance on the left. Neon and Argon are single atoms; oxygen and water are molecules made of more than one atom joined together.
  2. Press Solid, Liquid or Gas. The sim jumps straight to a temperature where that substance is in that state, so you do not have to wait.
  3. Watch the spacing between the particles in each state, and how fast they move.
  4. Use Heat and cool to change the temperature gradually instead. Drag it right to heat, left to cool. It springs back to off when you let go.
  5. Press Pause to stop everything so you can look carefully or take a screenshot, then press Play to carry on.
  6. Compare how much space the particles take up. In the solid and liquid states a dashed line marks the top of the material and the panel on the right tells you how much of the container is filled. Switch between Solid and Liquid and see which one needs more room. Try water, then try the others.
  7. Cool as far as the sim will go and watch the speed readout. See whether you can stop the particles moving altogether, and note the temperature when you do.

Tip: the temperature is shown in both kelvin and degrees Celsius, so you can answer questions in either unit.
