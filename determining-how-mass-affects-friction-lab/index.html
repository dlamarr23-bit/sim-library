How to use this simulation

  1. Tap hooked masses in the Load the tray tray to lay them on the tray. Use Remove last mass or Down to one to change the load. The tray itself is 400 g, and the scale needs at least one mass to hook.
  2. Press Pull the scale. The reading climbs steadily while the tray sits still, peaks right as it breaks free, then drops and holds steady while it slides.
  3. Watch the peak-hold ring stay at the highest reading even as the live needle falls back, and watch the chart under the bench trace the pull over time.
  4. Each pull is saved as a row in the Trials table and a point on both force graphs, so you can compare loads.
  5. Turn on Plot against normal force to read each graph's slope as the coefficient of friction, Add measurement error so repeated pulls don't land on the same number, or Slow motion to watch the needle drop closely.
  6. Press Reset to get ready for another pull, Clear trials to wipe the table and graphs, or Copy data to copy your results for a spreadsheet.
