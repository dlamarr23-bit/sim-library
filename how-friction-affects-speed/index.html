Model: the car leaves the ramp with speed v = √(2gh × 0.88) — the 0.88 accounts for friction on the ramp itself. On the flat surface it slows at a = μg, so distance d is covered in t = (v − √(v² − 2ad)) / a. Coefficients μ are typical values for a rolling toy car and are listed with each surface.
