Key vocabulary

  • Celsius scale — temperature scale where water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C
  • Exothermic reaction — releases energy in the form of heat
  • Endothermic reaction — absorbs energy in the form of heat
  • Chemical change — substances combine or break apart to form new substances
  • pH — a measure of how acidic or basic a substance is

  6. 6Hover over the graph to read exact temperatures at any time for both reactions at once.
